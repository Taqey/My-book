Lesson 1 Explanation & Concepts

Data Gathering & Sampling Bias: Behind the AI Models

Any intelligent system is only as smart as the raw data you feed into it. If your collection pipeline contains skewed samples, the model will output confident errors.

T

he mechanism of training machine learning systems depends on true representativeness. When we collect Primary Data via controlled field studies, we dictate the exact parameters.

However, relying purely on Secondary Data (e.g. scraped social feeds or uncurated public logs) exposes the model to silent systematic skewing.
